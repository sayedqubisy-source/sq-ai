Payments and subscription transactions may be processed by Paddle or another configured payment provider. SQ AI does not need to store your full payment card details in its own application database.

Account, usage and project data may be retained while your account is active and as reasonably necessary for security, billing, legal compliance and service operation.

We use reasonable technical measures such as hashed passwords, hashed session tokens, access controls and database protections. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices

You may contact SQ AI through the support channel provided by the application to request account or privacy assistance. Some records may need to be retained where required for security, billing or legal obligations.

8. Changes

This notice may be updated as the service changes. The latest version will be published on this page.
