Refund Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

1. Digital Service

SQ AI is a digital software service. Access to paid features may begin immediately after purchase, so refund eligibility depends on the circumstances of the charge and applicable law.

2. Refund Requests

If you were charged in error, were charged more than once, or experienced a significant service problem, contact SQ AI support as soon as reasonably possible with the account email and relevant transaction information.

3. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel a recurring subscription using the cancellation options made available by the service or payment provider. Cancellation normally prevents future recurring charges and does not automatically refund previous charges.

4. Failed Service and Credits

When a video generation job fails after credits were reserved, SQ AI is designed to return the reserved credit. Provider outages, queues, quotas, and safety refusals may affect generation availability.

5. Duplicate or Unauthorized Charges

If you believe a charge was duplicated or unauthorized, contact support promptly. You should also follow any applicable dispute or card-provider procedures.

6. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are normally returned through the original payment method where possible. Processing time may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

7. Paddle

Payments may be processed by Paddle. Paddle's applicable checkout, payment, and refund procedures may also apply to a transaction.

8. Contact

For billing or refund questions, use the support contact provided by SQ AI or in your purchase/checkout communications.
